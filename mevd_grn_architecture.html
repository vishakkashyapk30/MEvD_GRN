MEvD-GRN  ·  Multi-Evidence Distillation for GRN Inference

A compact dual-modality graph neural network that predicts directed TF → target regulatory edges from single-cell RNA + ATAC.

Task: directed bipartite link prediction  |  ~120K parameters  |  trains in minutes on one GPU

Model Architecture  (per-gene data flow)

scRNA-seq
per gene: mean & variance of
log-normalized expression
N_genes × 2
scATAC-seq
per gene: gene-activity score
(peaks within ±100 kb of TSS)
N_genes × 2
RNA Encoder
2-layer MLP · LayerNorm · GELU
→ N × 128
ATAC Encoder
linear projection · LayerNorm
→ N × 128
Gated Fusion
gate g = σ(W·[h_RNA ; h_ATAC])  →  h = g·h_RNA + (1−g)·h_ATAC
learns, per gene & per dimension, how much to trust each modality
N × 128
GNN Backbone — 2-layer GraphSAGE
bidirectional message passing over the prior candidate graph;
each gene aggregates regulatory context from its neighbors (2-hop)
context-aware embeddings · N × 128
Bilinear Decoder
score(TF, target) = σ( hTF · W · htargetT )
asymmetric W captures edge direction (TF→target ≠ target→TF)
edge probability ∈ [0, 1]
Output: ranked TF → target regulatory edges

Training Strategy — Multi-Evidence Curriculum

Supervised link prediction. The database's three evidence tiers form a natural confidence hierarchy; we train easy → hard, fine-tuning through each tier.

Stage 1 · Localization
ChIP-seq binding — many edges, noisy signal
EASY
fine-tune, lower learning rate
Stage 2 · Perturbation
knockout / CRISPR — functional & causal
HARDER
fine-tune, freeze encoders
Stage 3 · Dual-Evidence
binding AND functional — highest confidence
HARDEST
Why a curriculum? The highest-value tier (dual-evidence) is far too small to train from scratch. Earlier, larger tiers give a strong warm start, so the final stage only has to specialize. No existing GRN method uses these three tiers as a training curriculum — this is our core contribution.
Training details. AdamW + cosine-annealing LR · class-weighted BCE (focal option) · edge-level train/val/test split (no leakage) · hard negatives mined from adjacent tiers · early stopping on validation AUPR.
Evaluation. Score every candidate edge on a held-out tier, then rank. AUPR (primary — networks are sparse) · AUROC · Early Precision · EPR (enrichment over random).
Inputs: scRNA-seq + scATAC-seq  →  Ground truth: SC-MO-GRN-DB tiered reference networks GNN-based multimodal (RNA + ATAC) curriculum-trained
